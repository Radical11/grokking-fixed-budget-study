# Attribution and assistance

Generative AI (ChatGPT/Codex) assisted with experiment planning, Python and R code development, statistical-analysis implementation, manuscript drafting, and preparation of figures and presentation materials. Reported numerical results come from the saved training records and analysis outputs in this repository. The material is not represented as entirely unaided human work.

The project applies established ideas rather than claiming to invent grokking, restart theory, neural attention, AdamW or standard statistical tests:

- Power et al., [Grokking: Generalization Beyond Overfitting on Small Algorithmic Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177).
- Truong et al., [First-Passage Prediction of Grokking Delay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18845).
- Streeter, Golovin and Smith, [Restart Schedules for Ensembles of Problem Instances](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dgolovin/papers/aaai07b.pdf).
- Ootani, [Grokking Is Conditional and Fragile](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05104).
- Vaswani et al., [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).
- Loshchilov and Hutter, [Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101).

Full bibliographic records and the source/contribution discussion are under `paper/v2/`. The proposed contribution is the recorded equal-budget empirical comparison. Worldwide novelty, peer-review acceptance, an institutional similarity score and permission under any college AI-use policy are not claimed. The supplied syllabus informed the concept mapping; its PDF files are not redistributed in this public snapshot.
